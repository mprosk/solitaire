How to play

Build the six vertical stacks downward in immediate rank order, alternating colors. Aces and kings wrap: 2–A–K.

Move only a whole vertical stack. Its top card must fit beneath the bottom card of its destination. Play bottom cards into the up piles.

Up piles build upward by suit, one rank at a time, and wrap from king to ace. Start another suit with a card matching the first up card's rank. Cards may be taken back from the up piles.

The sideways pile can only play directly into an up pile. When its exposed card is moved, the next card turns over.

Turn the deck over one card at a time; there is no second pass. An empty vertical slot must be filled from the revealed deck pile before more cards can be turned. If the revealed pile is empty, the top deck card fills the slot directly.

Click the revealed card to fill the first empty slot. Double-click an eligible vertical or sideways card to send it to its up pile. Double-clicking the revealed card prefers its up pile, then an empty slot. Long-press the revealed card—or tap its count—to view every card in that pile.

You win once the deck is empty and both the revealed and sideways piles have no more than one card. You may return to the board and finish clearing it.
