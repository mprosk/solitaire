How to play

The deal is regular solitaire to start: seven columns, each with one card face up on top of its face-down cards (none in the first column, six in the last). Then the rest of the deck is dealt face up onto columns 2 to 7, four cards each.

Up top, build each suit's foundation up from its Ace to its King. Only the bottom card of a column can go up.

In the columns, build down by suit: a card goes on the next higher card of its suit (the 5♥ on the 6♥, an Ace on its 2), and only onto the bottom card of a column. Any face-up card can move, even from the middle of a column: the cards below it come along. When a face-down card is uncovered, it turns face up.

An empty column can only be filled by a King, with the cards below it. Twice a game, when you have no moves left, you can cheat: move a King, with the cards below it, to a new column, for up to nine. The counter at the top left shows the cheats you have left. With the game-over alert on, the new column appears by itself when you're stuck; with it off, tap the counter to bring it out.

Tap a card to move it, or drag it where it should go. You win when every card is up on the foundations. With the game-over alert on, once every card is face up and each column runs down by suit without a break, the rest goes up by itself. The game ends when no moves (or cheats) are left.
