How to play

Clear all 28 cards from the pyramid by removing pairs that add up to 13. An Ace counts as 1, a Jack 11 and a Queen 12. A King is 13, so it comes off on its own.

The top card and the bottom row are dealt face up, and the rows between them alternate face down and face up. A face-down card turns up once nothing covers it.

A card is free once nothing covers it. Pair two free cards, or a free card with the top card turned from the deck. A face-up card covered by only one card may also pair with the card covering it. Tap one card and then its partner, or drag one onto the other; tap a free King to remove it.

Tap the deck to turn three cards at a time. Only the top turned card is in play; pairing it away uncovers the one beneath. When the deck runs out, tap it to turn the cards back over. You may go through the deck as many times as you like.

You win when the pyramid is clear. The game ends when no more pairs can be made.
