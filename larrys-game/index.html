How to play

Get all 52 cards into the four suit columns. Each column holds one suit, in any order; rows line up by rank.

The face-up card dealt on its own starts the first column, and its rank is the key rank. Any card of the first column’s suit may be added to it at any time.

Start the next column with the key-rank card of another suit. A card can join a later column only if the card of the same rank is already in the column before it — that is, when the spot to its left is filled.

The four piles start with three cards each, and only the top card shows. When a pile is empty, you may move the top card of another pile into it.

Tap the deck to turn three cards at a time; only the top revealed card can be played. Go through the deck as many times as you like.

Tap a card to play it to its column (a pile card with no column move goes to an empty pile), or drag it. The game ends when every card is in a column or no legal moves remain.
