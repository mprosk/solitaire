How to play

Frame the board with all twelve face cards — Kings in the corners, Queens along the top and bottom, Jacks down the sides — and clear every other card away, leaving the four center spots empty.

Cards come off the deck one at a time. Face cards go straight to an open spot of their own. Number cards can go in any empty spot, including a face card’s, so place them with care: if a face card turns up and every spot for it is taken, the game is over.

Once all sixteen spots are full, clear pairs of number cards that add up to 10 (an Ace counts as 1). A 10 clears on its own. Tap one card and then its partner, or drag one onto the other. Then tap the deck to keep dealing into the gaps.

You go through the deck only once. When it runs out, clear whatever pairs are left. You win when only the face cards remain in the frame.
